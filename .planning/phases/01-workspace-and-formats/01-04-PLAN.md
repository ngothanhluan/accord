Purpose: FMT-07 and success criterion 4 — the seven templates are the human-facing half of the contract; the generated `templates` export is how core hands them to the CLI (`new ticket`, Phase 5) and to the MCP host (Phase 8) without a filesystem. The folder convention (FMT-01) is reflected in the templates' guidance.

Output: seven template files, `scripts/gen-templates.mjs`, committed `src/generated/templates.ts`, `templates` export, root `gen` script, `packages/core/test/templates.test.ts`.
