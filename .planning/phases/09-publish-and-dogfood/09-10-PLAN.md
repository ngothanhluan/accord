<objective>
Check the token rule against a real stylesheet before anyone proposes promoting it.

D-153 splits the dogfood across two repositories because no single one answers all four criteria.
This repository ships a CLI and no interface — every `.css` file here is a fixture or an example, and
`accord/config.yml`'s own comment records that the token rule reports as skipped. `C:/Work/SimplT`
answers the token half of ROADMAP criterion 4 and nothing else.

D-163 settles how. `tokenHardcoded` reads its *vocabulary* from `design.tokens` but scans only
`accord/assets/<id>/prototype.html` (`packages/core/src/lint/tokens.ts`, and the `PROTOTYPE` matcher
at `packages/core/src/load/snapshot.ts:23`). A stylesheet with no prototype yields zero findings for
a structural reason rather than a clean one — which would be a measurement of nothing. So the
measurement authors one prototype from a real screen's rendered markup, in a scratch worktree, and
lints it against the real stylesheet. A prototype is the artifact accord's own designer stage
expects, so this exercises the rule as designed rather than working around it.

D-161 settles what is reported: raw numbers and no conclusion. How many findings, how many are
genuinely hardcoded values, how many are false positives, with a concrete example of each. The
promotion decision belongs to v0.2, and a threshold set from a single stylesheet would be a decision
made on one data point.

Output: the last `pending` section of `09-VERIFICATION.md`, filled — and `C:/Work/SimplT` exactly as
it was found.
</objective>
