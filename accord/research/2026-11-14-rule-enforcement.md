### Method

2. Kept the 166 Must and Should lines (43 tickets). No Must or Should line carries `carried from`, so none was merged. Some records report the same defect again after an incomplete fix without the mark (e.g. `accord/tickets/BUG-LN-3/review.md:4` and `:9`; the four rounds of `accord/tickets/ST-LN-60/review.md`); they count as separate findings, which leaves the per-kind ticket counts unchanged.
3. Gave each finding one kind: the defect class a reviewer would name again in another ticket, at the level one rule or check could target. When a finding fits two kinds, the more specific cause won (a YAML spelling that skipped a gate is "frontmatter read by a regex", not "gate skipped"). 4. Counted, per kind, findings and distinct tickets. A kind is "repeated" when it appears in two or more tickets.
