# Release notes

Each section lists what changes for tickets in flight and for repositories pinned to an older accord. A release pull request copies the `## Unreleased` section into its `release:` commit body and renames the section to the release version.

## Unreleased

Each rule below that reads a pin has a cut-off constant in `packages/core/src/round/pin.ts` (`DISCUSS_FROM`, `APPROACH_CAP_FROM`, `GROUNDS_V2_FROM` and `PLAN_SIZE_FROM`), which holds the first release that ships the rule. The four read `0.4.1` while `v0.4.1` is not yet tagged.

- Discuss stage (from `DISCUSS_FROM`, 0.4.1): a ticket of lane M, L or P (every lane but S) claimed under that pin or later needs `accord/tickets/<id>/discuss.md`, with every gray area mapped to a Decision, before `accord gate ready` passes. The areas are the list items at the top level of a line (indented by up to three spaces and less than the text of the area before it: a sub-bullet indented as far as that text, two spaces under `- ` or three under `1. `, and any item indented four spaces or by a tab, is a note under the area; a paragraph after a blank line ends the list, so an item after it is top-level again) under the `## Gray areas` heading and under any heading that starts with `Gray areas`, such as `## Gray areas (cont.)`. A repeated or continued heading, and a line with a group and a Decision but no gray area text, fail the gate. A ticket claimed under an older pin, in flight when the repository upgrades, needs none.
- Approach critic (from `APPROACH_CAP_FROM`, 0.4.1): for a ticket of lane M, L or P the critic runs at most two rounds, and no Ruling raises the cap. A third round is refused with the findings still open. An open `risk: data-loss` or `risk: security` finding blocks the approval, in every round of such a ticket and when round 2 reports no findings or a verdict ends the loop: `accord show <id> --approval --short` lists it under `## Blocking findings`, and `accord approve` refuses with exit 1 and names it. This is a change in public behaviour: `accord approve` read no approach record before 0.4.1. The approach record is read strictly and fails closed. The one separator is ` — `; a hyphen, two hyphens or an en dash is part of the text of a state, a reason, a test or a command, so `command: npm test -- <path>` is never cut. A risk is read only from the finding's mark slot: a ` — `-separated segment after the finding's text that is `risk: security` or `risk: data-loss`, or `rests on: risk: security` or `rests on: risk: data-loss` (the critic's own wording), in any letter case, after the state or before it; or the `rests on:` line of the critic's block, the lines under a finding that are indented deeper than it, as `agents/critic.md` prints it. The block's other `rests on:` lines (`ac-<n>`, `D-<nn>`, a quote, `test:`, `command:`) are read as grounds, so a round 2 block with `rests on: ac-1` is not refused for grounds. Prose is never searched for a risk word: the finding's own words, a note under a `## Round` heading, the reason of a `withdrawn:` or `unverified:` state, the owner's words, a `No findings` line and a test name such as `risk:data-loss` are read as nothing, so they never refuse. A finding that names a risk only in prose, or in a line with no ` — `, is not a blocking finding; while it is open the short view lists it under `## Risks`. A mark slot is a segment after the finding's text, or a block line, that starts with a mark key (`risk`, `risks` or `rests on` followed by a colon, a semicolon or an equals sign), or is only `risk` and a kind, and a mark slot whose value does not parse is a record problem: `risks: security`, `risk: data_loss`, `risk; security`, a mark in backticks, quotes or asterisks, `risk: security.`, a hyphen that is not the ASCII one as in `data‑loss`, and `rests on: ac1` are problems. Every cycle of the record is held to this, and any other line of `approach-review.md` that accord cannot read is a record problem as well: `accord round --approach` refuses it, `accord approve` refuses with exit 1 naming the problems, and the short view lists them under `## Blocking findings`. Where a plan record follows the grounds rule (its first round is written under 0.4.1 or later), the same record problem refuses `accord round --plan` and, once a cycle holds three rounds, `accord gate plan` (rule `gate.plan-rounds`); `accord gate done` reads no plan record, and an older plan record is read as before. A ticket claimed under an older pin and a lane S ticket are asked none of this: `accord approve` still reads no record for them. A risk finding is open, whatever its severity, while its state is `open`, `unverified` or `deferred` (a risk finding is never deferred), while it is `repeated as` a finding that is not in a later round of the cycle, and while an `owner:` state has no words. A finding repeated as another, or repeated by a `repeats <round>.<n>:` line, passes its risk on, so a repeat line without a `risk:` mark blocks too. A risk finding stops blocking when it is `fixed`, `withdrawn`, repeated as a finding that exists, or dropped by the owner as `owner: "<words>"` (the short view then lists it under `## Risks` with the owner's words), and when any `## Closed` line (the owner's no with a Correction, `ruled out by`, `the owner picked`) starts a new cycle. A ticket claimed under an older pin keeps five rounds and its stop, even when its approach critique begins after the upgrade, so no round is lost; `accord approve` does not refuse it, and the short view lists its open risk findings under `## Risks`.
- Approval (from 0.4.1, a skill change shipped by `accord upgrade`): `accord-intent` asks no separate yes. The owner approves once, from `accord show <id> --approval --short`, which prints the Intent, one line per criterion, the Decisions, the risks and the ticket path. `accord show <id> --approval` prints the full sections as before.
- Repositories pinned to 0.4.0: a repository pinned to 0.4.0 keeps the 0.4.0 answers and skills until `accord upgrade`, which moves the pin and regenerates the skills. The discuss rule and the approach cap read the pin at the ticket's claim commit: the newest `claim: <id>` commit that is not on the default branch, else the newest one overall. A ticket with no `claim: <id>` commit has no claim commit to read, so it gets the new rules after the upgrade, wherever its ticket file was added. It reads the working-tree pin only when the ticket has no such commit or git has no HEAD. When git fails reading the pin, or `accord/config.yml` cannot be read at the claim commit (a blob a blobless clone never fetched), the new rules apply. Then `accord gate ready` names the git error in its discuss finding, `accord round --approach` names it in the refusals the cap rules give (past the cap, an ended loop, a blocked approval), `accord approve` names it when it refuses on a blocking finding, and `accord show <id> --approval --short` names it in its `## Blocking findings` part; the cap itself (the round number and the cap it prints) applies the new rules without naming it. Two rules follow the claim: the discuss rule and the approach cap with its approval block, so a ticket claimed before the upgrade keeps no discuss stage and five approach rounds until it is done. Two rules follow the record or the plan instead, and the upgrade changes them for such a ticket once a record or a plan begins after it: the grounds rule (round 2 and Must, `GROUNDS_V2_FROM`, and the plan fix scope) reads the pin at the first round of the record (`review.md`, `plan-review.md` or `approach-review.md`), and the plan size rule (`PLAN_SIZE_FROM`) reads the pin at the plan's baseline commit. The version 2 review record, in force since 0.3.1, is not changed by this release. A ticket claimed under 0.4.0 whose approach critique begins after the upgrade therefore keeps five rounds and its stop but is held to the round 2 grounds rule; one whose record began before the upgrade keeps the old grounds rule.
