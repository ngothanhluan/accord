# Glossary

<!-- BA. One term per entry: the word the team actually uses and its business meaning. Agents and tickets use these terms verbatim. -->

- **Weekly report** — the hours a team recorded between a Monday and the Sunday after it, already shown on the reports screen
- **Workspace member** — a person who records hours in this workspace and can open its reports screen
- **Report line** — one member's hours on one day, the row the reports screen shows and the export writes
