# Benchmark round 2, split per agent

Date: 2026-11-02. Repository at `0fcb8d13`. Feeds ST-LN-46 (scout and the shared harness), ST-LN-89 (executor), ST-LN-90 (planner) and ST-LN-85.

This is evidence, not a contract. Only a ticket's Intent, acceptance criteria, Vetoes, Decisions and Corrections bind any stage.

## The split

On 2026-11-02 the owner split benchmark round 2, which ST-LN-46 had grown to hold for every agent, into one ticket per agent. Owner (translated): "Scout: haiku and sonnet at low, medium, high. Executor: sonnet high and opus medium, or a new architecture is needed for the executor. Planner is certainly opus, only re-check the effort level, or a new architecture is needed. The other roles need discussing after implementing."

Why: each agent asks a different question with its own samples and scoring; one ticket per agent gives each its own acceptance criteria and lets each config change ship on its own evidence (quality > cost > speed).

- ST-LN-46 keeps the scout and builds the harness the others reuse.
- ST-LN-89: the executor.
- ST-LN-90: the planner, which since ST-LN-80 also drafts the Approach in place of the architect.
- Critic, reviewer, final reviewer, verifier, debugger and auditor: no ticket until the owner discusses them after the three above are built. Their notes are in the section below, and stay here until then.
