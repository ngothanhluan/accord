<user_story>
**As a** developer implementing a ticket that has passed Ready,
**I want to** follow one workflow that plans the change as vertical slices, reviews the plan
before writing code, and hands the finished change to a context that never wrote it,
**so that** the ticket carries independent evidence that the change does what the acceptance
criteria say — and I never have to remember the procedure myself.
</user_story>

<objective>
Ship the `dev` role: the workflow definition, the fresh-context review brief it hands off to,
and the two techniques that graduate out of `docs/skills/` into the package where the
renderer can reach them. Land the graduation and its replacement in one change, because the
author uses both drafts today and a window where neither exists is a real cost.

Purpose: this is the heaviest of the three workflows and the one the ROADMAP scope note is
about — the content that has to carry what a general planning system carried.
Output: `accord-dev/` rendered into this repository from source, with `docs/skills/` and the
two hand-maintained draft copies gone.
</objective>
