**As a** person turning an idea into a ticket an agent can implement,
**I want to** be walked from intent through EARS requirements and tagged Gherkin scenarios,
with a fresh context checking whether the criteria actually cover the intent before I claim
the ticket is ready,
**so that** no agent starts on a story whose acceptance criteria I only think are complete.
</user_story>
